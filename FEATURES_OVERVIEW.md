# V-POS Feature Overview

V-POS is an Android-ready, offline-first POS system built with Ionic, Angular, and Capacitor. It combines day-to-day selling, inventory control, staff management, and receipt printing in one mobile app.

## What It Can Do

### Fast POS Checkout
- Create sales with pickup or delivery categories.
- Assign up to 2 personnel per transaction.
- Handle refill and non-refill item pricing.
- Apply discounts and auto-calculate subtotal, VAT, and total.
- Support full or partial payments via Cash, GCash, and Bank Transfer.

### Sales and Credit Management
- Browse sales with date filtering, search, and pagination.
- View full sale details and line items.
- Accept follow-up balance payments on unpaid sales.
- Track credit/payment history.
- Reprint receipts and attach notes to transactions.

### Inventory and Warehouse Control
- Manage inventory by category with search and sorting.
- Add, edit, and remove products.
- Track low-stock items for store and warehouse.
- Manage disposals, commissions, and warehouse stocks.
- Keep item history records for stock movements.

### Transfer and Restock Workflows
- Create store and warehouse transfer documents.
- Handle convert, restock-in, and restock-out operations.
- Manage supplier-linked transfer flows.
- Cancel transfers with status tracking.

### Customer and Lend Tracking
- Maintain customer records with search and filters.
- Monitor customer balances and transaction history.
- Track lent items, lend history, and inactive customers.

### Personnel and Payroll Support
- Manage personnel records and role-based rate settings.
- View personnel transaction activity.
- Record salary-related history and payout operations.

### Petty Cash and Daily Operations
- Log petty cash entries with filtering and totals.
- Use dashboard analytics for sales, profit, expenses, and top items.
- View low-stock charts and operational summaries.

### Built-In Printing
- iMin printer integration for:
- POS receipts.
- Reprints.
- Balance slips.
- Salary slips.
- Lend slips.
- Business details printouts.

### Settings and App Tools
- Configure business/receipt information and logo.
- Use local SQLite data for offline-first operation.
- Use built-in login/session handling and guarded routes.
- Receive live-update prompts when a new bundle is available.

## Best Fit
V-POS is best suited for businesses that need reliable offline POS operations with strong inventory and personnel workflows, plus integrated receipt printing on Android devices.
